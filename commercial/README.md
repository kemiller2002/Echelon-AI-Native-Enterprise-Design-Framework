# Commercial strategy (Phase 7, not started)

The mission names three candidate products:

- **A. Discovery and Redesign:** a methodology plus consulting.
- **B. Architecture Platform:** software.
- **C. Transformation Runtime.**

No market research has been done in this repository, and nothing here is a
finding.

## Initial hypotheses to test (not conclusions)

| Id | Hypothesis | What would falsify it |
|---|---|---|
| HY-EEF-C01 | A (discovery and redesign) is the only offer that can be sold before more case studies exist: the output is a defensible, evidence-graded redesign. | No buyer pays for a redesign whose economics are "superior at expected only" |
| HY-EEF-C02 | B is differentiated by traceability and governance invariants (agents never hold human authority; controls never dropped), not by diagram generation. | Buyers rank generation speed above verifiability |
| HY-EEF-C03 | C should not be built until A and B have repeat customers. It is the most expensive offer and overlaps with workflow and agent platforms. | A design partner commits to fund a runtime pilot |

The daf-landscape repository already has a related research agenda
(`research/commercial-opportunity/2026-11-20-research-agenda.md`) covering DAF
buyers. Phase 7 should reuse its guardrails: no fabricated demand, pricing or
contacts, and no unsolicited outreach.
